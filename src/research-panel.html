What is measured, fitted, or still missing?
S14: the source Na/main ratio is 0.0893 ± 0.0223, and the AC-on/open ratio of ratios is 0.640 ± 0.186. The reference source mixture now uses their measured ratio. Fig. 5 uses the published 5C source abundances and the existing cross-talk extraction, including upper limits and marginal peaks.

The current engine uses the previously calibrated code geometry: RF ±70 V, AC ±0.35 V, inferred 7 mm gate aperture. S14 reports 83 kHz and AC 3 V; the drive convention and transfer to the rods remain unverified. Agreement here is a calibrated comparison, not an independent validation. Fig. 5 RF and DC settings are not fully reported.

Pixel bounds are not statistical confidence intervals. T/(T+C) is an ideal repeat-pass proxy, not the measured AC-on/open signal or collection time. No whole-trap delivery, space charge, or nine-channel competition is simulated. The recorded R31 scan and current mqviz runs use different engines; their results are not interchangeable.
